AI can now generate ten working versions of anything in seconds. It cannot tell you which one is good.

For years the hard part was making the thing. AI made that almost free. The hard part now is choosing, and that is a human skill called taste.

Taste is not personal preference. It is judgment trained on thousands of real outcomes until it is fast and reliable. And you cannot absorb it by reading polished AI output. It is built by doing the evaluating yourself.

So the edge is no longer what your team can build. A competitor can clone that in a weekend. The edge is what your team can tell apart.

Point AI at generation. Keep the judgment human.
