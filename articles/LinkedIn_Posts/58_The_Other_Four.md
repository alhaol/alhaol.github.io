A thousand years ago Ibn Sina listed six things that decide whether you stay healthy. Your smartwatch measures two of them.

The six: the air around you, what you eat and drink, sleep and waking, movement and rest, what your body takes in and lets go, and your emotional state.

Hold a fitness tracker against that list. Sleep, measured well. Movement, measured very well. Emotional state, guessed at from heart rate variability. Air, food, and what your body lets go, nothing at all.

Two measured, one inferred, three invisible.

Now the part worth noticing. The two your device measures are the two hardest to change. Sleep fights your job. Movement fights your calendar.

The four it ignores are the cheapest things on the list. Opening a window. Drinking water. Keeping email out of the bedroom. Putting an argument down before you sleep on it.

What gets measured gets managed. The quiet half of that rule is that whatever nobody measures gets ignored.
