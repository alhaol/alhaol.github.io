You wouldn't give a new team member the production deployment keys on day one.

You observe. You verify. You expand responsibility as trust builds. That's how trust works in teams—and it's exactly how trust should work with AI.

The principle is simple: match autonomy to risk. Low-stakes, reversible actions can run freely. High-stakes, irreversible ones require human judgment. The gradient between those two points is where intelligent governance lives.

Start conservative. Build a track record. Expand from there. Trust that can't be traced can't be relied upon.
