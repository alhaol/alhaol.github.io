Three agents draft your launch plan. You come back to forty pages and scroll to the bottom hoping.

You read 238 words a minute and hold about four things in mind. The agents write far faster than that.

So stop reading the stream. Have them draw a map at each checkpoint: budget, timeline, scope, promise. The one that drifted turns amber, and you fix it in one sentence.

Fill the map from real numbers, not the agent's summary.
