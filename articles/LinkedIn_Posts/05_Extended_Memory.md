A model's memory is finite. What you put in its context shapes what it can reason about.

The solution isn't a bigger window—it's smarter access. Instead of loading everything upfront, connect the AI to queryable systems that hold your documentation, your architecture, your history. It fetches what it needs, when it needs it.

The result: the model reasons over unlimited knowledge while staying focused on the actual problem.

Teams that treat their knowledge as queryable systems—not documents to be copy-pasted—will see compounding returns from every AI session.
