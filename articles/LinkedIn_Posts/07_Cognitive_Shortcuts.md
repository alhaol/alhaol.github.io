Every small decision—which test to run first, how to format a commit, which flag to pass—costs a fraction of your mental energy.

That fraction compounds. By the end of the day, the accumulated cost of micro-decisions leaves less capacity for the problems that actually matter.

The antidote: encode your best decisions once, so you never have to make them again. A well-designed command that encodes an expert workflow into a single keystroke isn't just faster—it frees your mind for harder problems.

Efficiency isn't about typing faster. It's about deciding less.
