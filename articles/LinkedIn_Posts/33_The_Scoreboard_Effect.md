A team plays the game its scoreboard rewards. AI is no different: a system becomes whatever its evaluation measures.

Most teams judge their AI by demos. A demo is a highlight reel. It shows the best five minutes and hides the fumbles.

Without a real scoreboard (clear tests, run on every change), progress is a feeling and regressions are invisible. And the scoreboard you pick decides the game: measure exam scores and you get an exam-taker. Measure finished tasks for real customers and you get a worker.

So before asking an AI team "how good is it?", ask "how do you keep score?" The second answer tells you more.
