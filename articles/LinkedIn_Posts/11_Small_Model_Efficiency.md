Picking the most accurate small language model for your pipeline is the wrong optimization.

New research benchmarks 15 SLMs across accuracy, speed, and energy consumption — and the findings should change how teams select models.

The most accurate model (Llama-3.2-1B) consumes 2.6× more energy per inference than the most efficient one (Phi-1.5B). The fastest model is not the most energy-efficient. And the "well-rounded" model — Mistral-7B — wins on neither axis but avoids losing on either. Every production deployment is a trade-off across three dimensions: correctness, computation, and consumption. Most teams only measure the first one.

For agentic AI systems running at scale, the energy footprint of inference compounds fast. Choosing the wrong SLM can double your operational costs and carbon footprint with no accuracy gain.

Measure all three axes before you deploy. The benchmarks now exist.
