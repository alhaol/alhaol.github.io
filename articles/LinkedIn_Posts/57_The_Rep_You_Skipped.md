A thousand years ago Ibn Sina named three reasons people forget things. Illness. Distraction. And laziness.

The third one is the only one your phone made worse.

Picture your memory as a library. There are shelves, where everything you have ever learned is filed. And there is a librarian, who walks the aisles and brings things back.

Shelves do not get out of shape. Librarians do.

An AI answers in about four hundred milliseconds. Reaching for the answer yourself takes two or three seconds. So the answer always lands first, and your librarian never gets up.

You do not lose the books. You lose the walk.

The fix is not to use AI less. It is to change who asks. Before you look up something you have read before, say your answer out loud first, even a bad one. That guess is the exercise.

Then let the machine check you instead of replacing you.
