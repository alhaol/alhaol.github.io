A sheep sees a wolf and runs. A thousand years ago Ibn Sina asked what the sheep actually saw.

Not a wolf. Light hit its eye carrying shape, colour and movement. That is all light can carry. The danger was never in the light.

So he invented a faculty for it, called wahm, estimation. It appears in no philosopher before him. Its job is to grasp the meaning the senses never delivered.

That is now the most useful thing you can know about AI.

A language model is the best form reader ever built. What it does not have is your afternoon. It was not in yesterday's meeting.

Ask what a two line message from your boss really means, and it reads the form beautifully, then fills in the meaning from the average of every similar message ever written. Sometimes that average is exactly wrong, and it will be wrong fluently.

So ask one question first. Does this answer need something that is not in the text I gave it?
