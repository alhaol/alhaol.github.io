More context doesn't always mean better output.

Research shows AI models—like humans—start degrading when asked to hold too much at once. They lose precision, miss buried details, fill gaps with plausible-sounding guesses.

The discipline isn't loading everything in. It's knowing what to keep, what to prune, and when to start fresh.

Clean context produces sharper thinking. That's true for engineers. It's equally true for AI.
