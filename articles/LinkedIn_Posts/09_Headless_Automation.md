Some engineering work is mechanical by nature. Dependency updates. Lint violations. Security patches. It doesn't require judgment—it requires execution.

When that work lands in an engineer's sprint, it crowds out the problems that actually need human thinking.

The alternative: automate the preparation, keep the approval. AI handles the mechanical work—detects issues, proposes fixes, runs tests, creates a pull request. The engineer decides whether to merge.

The tedious work still gets done. The engineer stays focused on what requires them.
