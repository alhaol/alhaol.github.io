The single biggest constraint in engineering isn't skill—it's attention.

You can only hold one problem deeply in your head at a time. Switching between workstreams is expensive, even for experienced engineers.

The breakthrough: you don't have to switch. Multiple AI-assisted workstreams can run in parallel, each focused on its own domain, while you hold the architectural view across all of them.

One engineer. Multiple streams. No context loss. The math changes when attention stops being the bottleneck.
