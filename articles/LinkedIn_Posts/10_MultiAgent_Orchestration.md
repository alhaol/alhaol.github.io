A single perspective on a complex problem will always have blind spots.

In machine learning, ensembles consistently outperform single models—not because each model is smarter, but because different perspectives cancel out each other's errors. The same logic applies to architecture design.

When multiple AI agents explore the same problem through different lenses—one optimizing for simplicity, one for resilience, one for scale—their differences surface the trade-offs that matter.

The goal isn't to find the one right answer faster. It's to understand the decision space well enough to make the right choice.
