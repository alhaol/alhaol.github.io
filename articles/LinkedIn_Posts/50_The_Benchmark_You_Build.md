Hugging Face retired its own Open LLM Leaderboard in March 2025, after scoring more than 13,000 models.

Not because it was wrong. Because it had stopped measuring what the field actually cared about.

That is the part most teams miss. A public score does not go false. It goes irrelevant, and nothing on the page tells you when that happened.

Arabic makes it obvious. Almost every Arabic benchmark is written in Modern Standard Arabic. Your users type Gulf or Levantine dialect and switch to English for technical terms. No public leaderboard covers that square, and it is the only square you are paid to be correct in.

So build it yourself. Five hundred items pulled from real logs, not from imagination. Labelled twice, with the disagreement rate written down, because that rate is your ceiling. Half of it kept private and never pasted into a vendor console.

Then every claim, internal or from a vendor, gets restated as a score on your set.

Model weights are a commodity that changes every few months. The evaluation set is the part that appreciates.
