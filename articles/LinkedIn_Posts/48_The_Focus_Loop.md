Focus doesn't break when you get interrupted. It breaks in the 23 minutes it takes to find your place again.

Most focus apps chase streaks. The real fix is smaller: let AI hold your task's state and hand you one tiny next step when you sit back down. A broken session becomes a 20 second tax instead of a lost hour.

Try it this week. Two prompts, one at the start of a work block and one at the end, are all it takes. Full method in the article below.
