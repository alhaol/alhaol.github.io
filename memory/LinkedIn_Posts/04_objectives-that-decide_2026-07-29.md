Five objectives for the quarter, and not one of them conflicts with any other. That reads like alignment.

It is the clearest sign that none of the five is working as an objective. An objective is not a direction you point in. It is a sentence that settles an argument you have not had yet.

The test is one line: to do this, we are not doing ______. If you cannot fill that blank with something a real person actually asked you for, you are holding a wish. Wishes are fine. They just do not belong on a list anyone is measured against. Worth noticing that SMART checks five things about an objective and not one of them asks what it costs, which is how a perfectly SMART objective stays a wish.

The page walks you into the trap where every individual call is defensible, you can narrate all of them, and the quarter still ends with five things half moved. Then it hands you a four row card for your own quarter.
