# Why Your Shot Did Not Land

Your kid says the game stole the kill. They are probably right, just not in the way anyone assumes.

Online games run on three clocks, not one. Your screen has a frame rate. The server has a tick rate. The wire between them has a ping. Only the server clock decides who got the kill, and to stay fair it does something strange: when you shoot, it winds the whole match backwards by roughly your ping and checks where everyone was when your screen drew the frame you aimed at.

That rewind is also the trap. It is why you can die after you reach cover on your own screen, while the killcam shows you standing in the open. A faster monitor does not fix it. Understanding it changes how you hold an angle.

I wrote it up as a memory class for teenagers: three clocks, one diagram, the trap, and the twenty line game loop underneath all of it.
