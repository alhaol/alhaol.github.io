# Presentation Outline: Agentic Workflow to Enhance Teaching Outcomes

## Section 1: The Context of Agentic AI

- **Slide 1: Title Slide** * _Title:_ Agentic Workflow to Enhance Teaching Outcomes
    
    - _Subtitle:_ Empowering Education through Autonomous, Iterative AI Systems
        
- **Slide 2: Section Divider** * 01: The Context of Agentic AI
    
- **Slide 3: What is an Agentic Workflow?** * Moving beyond simple zero-shot AI prompting to continuous, iterative problem-solving.
    
    - Mimicking deep cognitive processes to guide students, rather than just giving answers.
        
- **Slide 4: Four Pillars of Agentic AI** * _Reflection:_ Self-correcting and refining feedback.
    
    - _Tool Use:_ Autonomously accessing LMS, search, and code interpreters.
        
    - _Multi-Agent:_ Specialized agents collaborating (e.g., math and reading agents).
        
- **Slide 5: Overcoming Bloom's 2 Sigma Problem** * Scaling 1:1 personalized instruction to unlock the 98% performance potential of tutored students.
    

## Section 2: Core Applications in Teaching

- **Slide 6: Section Divider** * 02: Core Applications in Teaching
    
- **Slide 7: Personalized Tutoring Agents** * Moving beyond static chatbots to proactive, adaptive AI that manages cognitive load and micro-syllabi.
    
- **Slide 8: Autonomous Grading & Feedback** * _Multi-Step Rubric Analysis:_ Cross-referencing rubrics and peer-reviewing grades.
    
    - _Formative Feedback Loops:_ Shifting from summative scoring to actionable, iterative improvement steps.
        
- **Slide 9: Dynamic Curriculum Generation** * _Analysis:_ Parsing class-wide data for knowledge gaps.
    
    - _Generation:_ Autonomous planning of tailored modules.
        
    - _Adaptation:_ Adjusting difficulty dynamically during lessons.
        
- **Slide 10: The Paradigm Shift** * Quote from Andrew Ng on the shift toward delegating complex workflows to AI agents.
    

## Section 3: Insights & Implications

- **Slide 11: Section Divider** * 03: Insights & Implications
    
- **Slide 12: Teachers as Orchestrators** * Educators shift from grading to reviewing analytics and focusing on high-impact human interventions (empathy, mentorship).
    
- **Slide 13: Exponential Reach of Interventions** * Chart demonstrating how agentic AI scales personalized help over time, removing human bottlenecks.
    
- **Slide 14: Ethical & Practical Implications** * _Efficacy Validation:_ Need for continuous A/B testing.
    
    - _Data Privacy & Security:_ Protecting student cognitive history.
        
    - _Mitigating Algorithmic Bias:_ Auditing multi-agent systems.
        
    - _AI Literacy Curriculum:_ Training students and teachers to manage agents.
        
- **Slide 15: Closing the Achievement Gap** * Chart showing how Agentic AI dramatically raises the floor for the bottom quartile of students by providing infinite patience.
